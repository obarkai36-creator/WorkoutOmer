For him  9/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
A dessert that behaves like a protein snack. 20 g protein for just 160 kcal (~12.5 g protein per 100 kcal), 8 g fiber, 0 g sugar, ~4 g net carb — even leaner than the Quest bar for the same protein, and it scratches a sweet/pastry craving. Standout craving-killer on a cut. Same caveats as the other engineered snacks: ultra-processed, leans on erythritol (~8 g) + sucralose; collagen is an incomplete protein but it's blended with complete dairy protein so the amino profile is fine.
Highlights
  • 20 g protein / 160 kcal (best protein-per-calorie of the snacks)
  • 8 g fiber
  • 0 g sugar, ~4 g net carb
  • sugar alcohols + sucralose
  • ultra-processed
Suggested modifications
  • Ideal swap for when you want something sweet/dessert-like without the sugar hit
  • 1 pack at a time — erythritol can bloat if stacked
  • Slightly better cut pick than the Quest bar on calories; Quest still wins on fiber (14 g vs 8 g)

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Good high-protein, fiber-containing sweet option for her — 20 g protein, 8 g fiber, no sugar. Not a prenatal micronutrient contributor. Sweeteners (erythritol, sucralose) are generally regarded safe in pregnancy in normal amounts; moderate if she prefers to limit them. Contains milk and egg.
Highlights
  • 20 g complete (dairy+egg) protein
  • 8 g fiber
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Erythritol + sucralose; considered safe in pregnancy in normal amounts. Moderate if limiting non-nutritive sweeteners. — swap: whole-food snack if avoiding sweeteners
Suggested modifications
  • Occasional sweet/protein top-up; pair with fruit for prenatal micros
  • Allergens: milk, egg
