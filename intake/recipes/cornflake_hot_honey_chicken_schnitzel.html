Ingredients

  • chicken breast, cut in strips300 g
  • crushed garlic1 tsp
  • smoked paprika1 tsp
  • salt, pepperto taste
  • flour3 tbsp (dredge)
  • egg1 (dredge)
  • crushed cornflakes1.5 cups (~42 g, the crust)
  • honey (sauce)2 tbsp
  • sriracha (sauce)1 tbsp
  • soy sauce (sauce)1 tsp
  • garlic powder (sauce)1 tsp

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Cut the chicken into even strips and toss with the crushed garlic, smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Set up 3 bowls: flour / beaten egg / crushed cornflakes. Dredge each strip flour → egg → cornflakes, pressing the cornflakes on for a thick, even crust. (Crush the cornflakes coarse, not to powder — that's what makes the crunch.)
  3. Lay the strips on a lined tray; for all-around crisp without frying, set them on a wire rack over the tray and lightly spray/brush with oil.
  4. Bake at 200 C (400 F) for 18–20 min, flipping halfway, until golden and cooked through (74 C, no pink).
  5. Meanwhile simmer the honey, sriracha, soy sauce and garlic powder 1–2 min on medium until glossy.
  6. Brush the glaze on AFTER baking (or toss just before serving) — glazing before baking would burn the sugar and soften the crust.
  7. Tip: cornflakes give a lighter, crunchier crust than breadcrumbs; add the sauce at the last moment to keep it crisp.

For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Impressively lean for a schnitzel — it's BAKED, not fried, so the whole dish has only 13 g fat while packing 80 g protein (~9.2 g protein per 100 kcal). The catch is carbs: ~105 g from the cornflake + flour crust and the honey glaze (~34 g of that is honey sugar). Split into 2 it's ~435 kcal / 40 g protein / 52 g carb each — a solid protein-forward meal. The honey glaze is the main sugar lever; the crust is refined carbs.
Highlights
  • 80 g protein, only 13 g fat (baked not fried)
  • ~9.2 g protein per 100 kcal
  • carb-heavy (~105 g) — cornflake/flour crust + honey
  • ~34 g added sugar (honey glaze)
  • high sodium (~1,400 mg/dish; ~700 if split)
Suggested modifications
  • Split the dish into 2 servings for logging — 870 kcal is a big single meal
  • Cut the honey to 1 tbsp (halves the added sugar) — the sriracha still carries it
  • Use low-sodium soy; serve over salad/greens for fiber the crust lacks
