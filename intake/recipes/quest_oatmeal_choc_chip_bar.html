For him  9/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Just about the ideal cut snack. 20 g protein for 180 kcal (~11 g protein per 100 kcal) AND 14 g fiber — nearly half your 32 g/day target in one bar — with just 1 g sugar and ~5 g net carb. High protein + high fiber = strong satiety on a deficit, and it's portable. This is the fiber the protein puffs lacked, so the two complement each other well. Minor notes only: it's ultra-processed and leans on sugar alcohols (erythritol/glycerin) + sucralose/stevia — fine for most, but the sugar alcohols can cause bloating/GI if you eat more than one.
Highlights
  • 20 g protein / 180 kcal
  • 14 g fiber (huge — ~44% of daily target)
  • 1 g sugar, ~5 g net carb
  • modest sodium (210 mg)
  • sugar alcohols + sucralose (GI/sweetener note)
Suggested modifications
  • Best used to rescue a low-protein OR low-fiber day — it fixes both at once
  • Cap at 1 bar most days; sugar alcohols (5 g) can bloat if you stack them
  • Pair the two rotation snacks by purpose: puffs for pure protein, this bar when you also need fiber

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
A good high-protein, high-fiber snack for her too — 20 g protein and 14 g fiber (fiber genuinely helps pregnancy-related constipation), with almost no sugar. Not a prenatal micronutrient contributor (no meaningful folate/iron/choline). Sweeteners (sucralose, stevia) and erythritol are generally regarded as safe in pregnancy in normal amounts; some prefer to moderate. Contains milk, almond, and soy lecithin — note if relevant to her.
Highlights
  • 20 g complete (dairy) protein per bar
  • 14 g fiber — helpful for pregnancy constipation
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Contains sucralose + stevia + erythritol. Considered safe in pregnancy in normal amounts per FDA; moderate if she prefers to limit non-nutritive sweeteners. — swap: whole-food protein/fiber snack if she'd rather avoid sweeteners
Suggested modifications
  • Fine as an occasional protein/fiber top-up; pair with fruit for prenatal micros
  • Allergens: milk, almond (tree nut), soy lecithin
