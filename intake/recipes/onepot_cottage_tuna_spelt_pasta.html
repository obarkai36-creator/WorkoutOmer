🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. For more depth, soften the sliced red onion + garlic in a little oil in the wide pot first (optional but worth it).
  2. Add the dry spelt pasta, drained tuna, pepper, chili and 2–2.5 cups water to the pot; bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook uncovered, stirring often so the pasta doesn't stick, until the pasta is al dente and most of the water is absorbed (~10–12 min). Spelt can go from firm to soft quickly, so check early; add a splash more water if it dries before the pasta is done.
  4. Lower the heat, then stir in the cottage cheese and grated yellow cheese until melted and creamy — keep it gentle (hard-boiling cottage can turn it grainy).
  5. TASTE before adding the 1.5 tsp salt — the cottage, cheese and tuna are already very salty, so you'll likely need much less or none. This is the single biggest lever on the dish.
  6. Rest 2–3 min off the heat to thicken; finish with fresh herbs and chili.
  7. Tip: stir a couple of handfuls of spinach or some peas in at the end for folate + fiber.

A seriously protein-packed comfort pasta — ~52 g protein for ~582 kcal (~9 g protein per 100 kcal) from cottage + tuna + yellow cheese, with spelt's whole-grain fiber (~6 g/serving) and big calcium. The spelt swap already improved it (more protein/fiber, fewer refined carbs). The one thing dragging it down hard is SODIUM: ~1,500 mg/serving (≈65% of a day's limit) from 1.5 tsp added salt stacked on already-salty cottage, cheese and canned tuna.
Highlights

Suggested modifications
  • Skip or halve the added salt — the cottage/cheese/tuna already bring plenty; taste first. Biggest lever by far
  • Stir in spinach or peas at the end for folate/fiber and to lighten the carb share
