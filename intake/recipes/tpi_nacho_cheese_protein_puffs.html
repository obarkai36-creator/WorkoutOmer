For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
An elite snack on macros: 21 g protein for 130 kcal is ~16 g protein per 100 kcal — about as protein-dense as food gets — with near-zero carb, no sugar, low fat, and a complete milk-protein amino profile (~1.8 g leucine/serving, right at the muscle-protein-synthesis threshold). Ideal for hitting protein on a cut. Two knocks keep it off a 9: ~390 mg sodium/serving (17% DV — watch multi-cup grazing) and it's ultra-processed with 0 fiber and no sperm-priority micros.
Highlights
  • extreme protein density (~16 g/100 kcal)
  • complete protein, ~1.8 g leucine/serving
  • near-zero carb / no sugar
  • high sodium (390 mg/serving)
  • ultra-processed, 0 fiber
Suggested modifications
  • Great as a 1-cup protein-topper on a salad (adds crunch + protein without carbs) — the label's own suggestion, and it fixes the 0-fiber issue by pairing with greens
  • Cap at ~1–2 cups so sodium stays reasonable
  • Strong candidate to adopt as a go-to high-protein snack — say the word and I'll flag it

For her  6/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
A fine high-protein, high-calcium snack for her — 21 g complete protein plus 280 mg calcium (20% DV) and a little vitamin D, all genuine preconception positives. But it's ultra-processed, high-sodium, and brings no folate or iron, so it's a protein/calcium top-up rather than a nutrient-dense choice. No pregnancy safety flags (dairy is pasteurized). Note: contains milk.
Highlights
  • 21 g complete protein per 30 g serving
  • calcium 280 mg (20% DV) + vitamin D 1.4 mcg — bone/prenatal support
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Ultra-processed, high sodium (390 mg/serving) — fine in moderation; keep portions modest during pregnancy. — swap: pair with fresh veg; limit to 1 cup
Suggested modifications

  • Pair with fruit or veg to add the fiber/folate it lacks
