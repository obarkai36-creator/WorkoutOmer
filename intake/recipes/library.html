Suggested modifications

Highlights

  • choline from the 2 eggs — commonly under-consumed preconception
Suggested modifications
  • Fold a couple of handfuls of chopped spinach into the mix — adds folate and more iron
  • Serve with a citrus or tomato side to boost non-heme iron absorption

For him  6/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Excellent protein density (~5.6 g protein per 100 kcal) from the lean poultry + bulgur, and real whole-grain fiber. But the ⅓ cup olive oil, THREE in-mix sweeteners (honey + silan + maple) plus a maple/pomegranate sauce push it to a calorie-dense, sugar-heavy (~19 g added sugar/serving) meal — the weak spot on a cut. Sodium is very high (~900 mg/serving from 1 tbsp salt). A 6 as written; an easy 8 with the trims below.
Highlights
  • very high protein density
  • whole-grain fiber (bulgur)
  • high added sugar (~19 g/serving)
  • high sodium (~900 mg/serving)
  • calorie-dense from ⅓ cup oil
Suggested modifications
  • Cut olive oil from ⅓ cup to ~2 tbsp — lean poultry doesn't need it (saves ~350 kcal / ~40 g fat across the batch)
  • Drop the three in-mix sweeteners (honey/silan/maple); keep only the sauce for the caramel effect — halves the added sugar
  • Cut salt from 1 tbsp to ~1.5 tsp — 900 mg sodium/serving is a lot for one dish
  • Serve over cauliflower rice or a big salad instead of white rice to hold the deficit and add fiber/lycopene/vitamin C
  • Finish with pomegranate seeds (the garnish) — genuine vitamin C + polyphenols, sperm-friendly

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Solid preconception base — lean poultry protein plus iron (~1.7 mg), choline (~55 mg), zinc, and bulgur's whole-grain folate/fiber. Folate is only modest as-is but trivially boosted with more herbs / a legume side. Naturally pairs iron with the pomegranate's vitamin C (better non-heme absorption). No unsafe ingredients — just cook the ground poultry through.
Highlights
  • iron from the poultry + bulgur, paired with pomegranate vitamin C for absorption
  • choline from the poultry
  • whole-grain folate + fiber from the bulgur
  • parsley/cilantro add folate — stir some in fresh at the end, not only cooked
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Ground poultry must be cooked fully through (no pink, ~74°C internal) — standard for meatballs, just don't undercook the centers. — swap: cook to firm/steaming
  • ⚠ High sodium (~900 mg/serving) — worth easing during pregnancy (BP/edema). — swap: cut added salt to ~1.5 tsp
Suggested modifications
  • Double the fresh herbs and fold half in raw at the end for more folate
  • Serve with a lentil or leafy-green side — adds folate + iron, and the pomegranate's vitamin C boosts absorption
