🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Prep everything first — stir-fry moves fast. Soak the glass noodles in warm water until pliable (5-10 min), drain, and snip shorter with scissors.
  2. Steam or blanch the broccoli ~2 min (it's the densest veg), then drain — this cooks it evenly and keeps it bright and crisp-tender.
  3. Pat the chicken strips dry and season lightly. Heat the pan/wok hot before the oil goes in — high heat sears, low heat steams.
  4. Stir-fry the chicken in a single layer until just cooked, then remove (crowding makes it grey and watery).
  5. Stir-fry the garlic and onion briefly, then the mushrooms — leave the mushrooms undisturbed ~1 min to brown (moving them too soon makes them release water and steam).
  6. Add the zucchini and blanched broccoli and toss 2-3 min to keep them crisp-tender.
  7. Return the chicken, add the noodles with a splash of water, the sesame oil and 1 tbsp soy sauce; toss 1-2 min to combine and heat through (taste before adding any more salt).
  8. Fold in the spinach at the very end and toss just until wilted — seconds only; overcooking dulls it and loses folate.
  9. Finish off the heat with a squeeze of lemon or lime — it brightens everything and boosts iron absorption. Serve promptly.
