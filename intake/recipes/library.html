For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
High protein density (~8.8 g protein / 100 kcal), low sugar, veg baked in — a strong cutting/muscle-retention dinner. Fat is a touch high from the frying, and fiber is low.
Highlights
  • high protein density
  • hidden vegetables
  • moderate zinc + selenium (sperm micros)
Suggested modifications
  • Swap ½ cup breadcrumbs for oat bran or ground oats to lift fiber toward the 32 g/day target
  • Bake or air-fry instead of pan-frying to cut the added fat
  • Serve over a big tomato/leafy salad — adds lycopene, vitamin C and fiber the dish itself is short on

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Good lean protein plus iron and choline (from the eggs) — both preconception priorities. Folate is low as-is; easy to fix with greens. Fully cooked, no safety flags.
Highlights
  • iron from the chicken (pair with a vitamin-C side for absorption)
  • choline from the 2 eggs — commonly under-consumed preconception
Suggested modifications
  • Fold a couple of handfuls of chopped spinach into the mix — adds folate and more iron
  • Serve with a citrus or tomato side to boost non-heme iron absorption
