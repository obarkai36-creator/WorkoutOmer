🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. In a bowl, mix the spelt flour, baking powder and salt. Add the yogurt, olive oil and just ¼ cup cold water to start — spelt absorbs water fast, so add more only if the dough is too dry.
  2. Stir to a shaggy dough, then knead GENTLY for ~1 min — spelt has weaker gluten than wheat, so it's softer and tears more easily; over-kneading makes it gummy. Stop as soon as it comes together.
  3. Cover and rest 10-15 min so the flour fully hydrates — this makes the soft dough much easier to roll.
  4. Divide into 8-9 balls. On a lightly floured surface (don't over-flour), roll each ~½ cm thick.
  5. For the pizza version: spread a little pizza sauce and cheese on one half, fold over (or top a second round) and press the edges to seal.
  6. Heat a pan over medium with a thin film of olive oil. Cook each pitta ~2-3 min per side until deep golden with brown spots and the cheese is melted — medium heat so the inside cooks before the outside burns.
  7. Keep the finished pittas under a clean towel so they stay soft.
  8. Tip: spelt browns a touch faster than white flour — watch the heat and pull them at deep golden, not dark.
