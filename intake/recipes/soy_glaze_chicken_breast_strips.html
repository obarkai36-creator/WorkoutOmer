🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Cut the breast into even, plump strips — uniform thickness is the #1 factor for juicy chicken — and pat them dry.
  2. Mix the marinade until smooth and coat the chicken; the cornflour is the magic ingredient — it locks in moisture and forms a light glaze. Marinate 20-30 min if you have time.
  3. Heat a little olive oil in a pan over medium (not high — high heat dries chicken out).
  4. Lay the strips in a single layer and leave undisturbed ~4 min — they release on their own when a golden crust forms; if they stick, they're not ready to flip.
  5. Flip and cook 3-4 min more to 74 C internal, then pull them just as they're done.
  6. Rest 5 minutes before serving — the fibers relax and reabsorb the juices (skipping this is why chicken goes dry).
  7. Tip: don't crowd the pan; cook in two batches so the strips sear instead of steam.

For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
A genuinely strong high-protein main — 46 g protein for ~380 kcal (~12 g protein per 100 kcal), lean chicken kept juicy by the cornflour trick (smart: moisture without much cost). Two levers to watch, both easy: SODIUM is high (~830 mg/serving from 3 tbsp soy + ½ tsp salt + sweet chili), and fat is moderate (~17 g) because the marinade carries 3 tbsp olive oil + sesame oil on top of the frying oil. Added sugar is modest (~5 g/serving from maple + sweet chili).
Highlights
  • very high protein (46 g/serving)
  • lean chicken, cornflour moisture-lock
  • high sodium (~830 mg/serving)
  • fat driven by marinade oils (~17 g)
  • modest added sugar (~5 g)
Suggested modifications
  • Use low-sodium soy or cut it to 2 tbsp — biggest sodium lever
  • Halve the marinade olive oil (1.5 tbsp) — it's mostly flavor/coating; saves ~5 g fat/serving with little loss
  • Serve over a big salad or veg to add the fiber/micros the dish itself lacks
