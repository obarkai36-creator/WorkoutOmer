Ingredients: chicken breast, spelt pasta, olive oil, cherry tomatoes, Mutti tomato paste, egg, cornflour, salt, sugar

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Boil the spelt pasta in well-salted water to just under al dente (it finishes in the sauce); scoop out a cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Pat the chicken strips dry, then coat in beaten egg + cornflour — the dry surface and starch give a light crust that keeps the chicken juicy.
  3. Sear the chicken in the olive oil over medium-high, undisturbed, until golden and just cooked (74 C); remove and set aside so it doesn't overcook.
  4. In the same pot, cook the cherry tomatoes and tomato paste with a pinch of salt and the 1/2 tsp sugar 5-8 min until jammy — the sugar balances the tomato acidity.
  5. Loosen the sauce with a splash of pasta water, then fold in the pasta and chicken and toss 1-2 min so the pasta drinks up the flavor.
  6. Taste, adjust salt and pepper, finish with fresh herbs.
  7. Tip: measure the olive oil (~20 g) — it's the single biggest calorie swing in the dish.
