🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Whip the egg whites to soft, glossy, cloud-like peaks — not stiff and dry (overwhipping makes the cookies dry).
  2. Gently fold the whites into the almond flour, sugar, lemon zest, juice and vanilla until you get a sticky dough that clings to the spoon — sticky means chewy.
  3. Chill the dough 20-30 min so it's easy to roll.
  4. Roll into small balls, then roll each generously in granulated sugar (regular sugar gives a crisper, more crackled shell than powdered).
  5. Set them on a lined tray and leave out ~30 min before baking — drying the surface gives the signature big cracks.
  6. Bake at 160 C (325 F) for ~12-15 min: lower and shorter keeps a soft, marzipan-like centre with a light crust. Pull them when the cracks show and edges are just set — they firm as they cool.
  7. Cool on the tray a few minutes before moving; they're fragile while warm.
  8. Tip: a few drops of almond extract deepen the classic amaretti flavor.

Suggested modifications
  • Cut the granulated sugar by ~⅓ — amaretti hold together fine with less; drops each cookie toward ~90 kcal / ~8 g sugar
  • Portion-cap at 1–2 with coffee rather than grazing the batch
  • Skip the powdered-sugar roll to shave a little more sugar
