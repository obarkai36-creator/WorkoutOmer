For him  7/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Nutritionally one of the best mains here — 44 g protein/serving (~9.2 g protein per 100 kcal), a huge load of green veg, ~5 g fiber/serving (32 g across the batch), plus real folate/iron/vitamin C. The single thing dragging it from a 9 to a 7 is SODIUM: 8 tbsp of soy total (~1,133 mg/serving — roughly half a day's limit in one plate). Fat is moderate (~14 g from 3–4 tbsp olive + sesame oil); glass noodles are refined but well-balanced by all the veg.
Highlights
  • 44 g protein/serving
  • very veg-dense, ~5 g fiber/serving
  • folate + iron + vitamin C from spinach/broccoli
  • VERY high sodium (~1,133 mg/serving, 8 tbsp soy)
  • refined glass noodles
Suggested modifications
  • Use low-sodium soy and cut the total soy to ~4–5 tbsp — this is the one real fix; it nearly halves the sodium
  • Trim frying oil toward 2 tbsp to shave fat
  • Otherwise excellent as-is — one of the highest-veg, highest-fiber mains in the library

For her  8/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Excellent for her — arguably the best preconception main analyzed. Spinach + broccoli + green beans stack up serious FOLATE (~80 mcg DFE/serving after cooking, the #1 preconception nutrient), IRON (~3.2 mg) that's naturally paired with the dish's own vitamin C (~42 mg) for better absorption, plus lean protein + choline. Fully cooked, no safety flags. Only caveat: the sodium — ease it with low-sodium soy.
Highlights
  • high folate from spinach + broccoli + green beans (top preconception nutrient)
  • iron (~3.2 mg) paired with the dish's own vitamin C (~42 mg) for absorption
  • lean protein + choline; broad green-veg micros
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ High sodium (~1,133 mg/serving from 8 tbsp soy) — worth easing in pregnancy. — swap: low-sodium soy, cut total soy to 4–5 tbsp
Suggested modifications
  • This is a keeper for her preconception nutrients — just dial down the soy
  • Serve promptly so the folate-rich greens aren't overcooked/held too long
