For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
An excellent macro cereal — 11 g protein AND 9 g fiber for just 110 kcal, with 0 g sugar and ~7 g net carb. Same protein+fiber role as the Quest bar, but as a cereal: pour milk or Greek/PRO yogurt over it and you push protein higher AND complete the amino profile (the base is plant pea protein, slightly lower in methionine on its own). Great low-cal, high-satiety breakfast/snack. Knocks: ultra-processed, moderate sodium (~190 mg), pea protein less complete than dairy solo.
Highlights
  • 11 g protein + 9 g fiber for 110 kcal
  • 0 g sugar, ~7 g net carb
  • plant (pea) protein — pair with dairy to complete it
  • moderate sodium (~190 mg)
  • ultra-processed
Suggested modifications
  • Eat with PRO yogurt or milk — adds protein and completes the amino profile
  • Great as a low-cal high-fiber breakfast on a cut, or a crunchy yogurt topping
  • Watch total servings — it's easy to pour more than one ½-cup serving

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
A good high-protein, high-fiber option for her — 11 g protein and 9 g fiber (fiber helps pregnancy constipation), 0 sugar, plus a little plant iron. Stevia and monk fruit are non-nutritive sweeteners generally regarded as safe in pregnancy. Not a big prenatal micronutrient source. Contains peanuts (and made in a facility with milk/tree nuts) — note if relevant.
Highlights
  • 11 g protein + 9 g fiber per serving
  • a little plant iron (pair with a vitamin-C fruit to absorb it)
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Sweetened with stevia + monk fruit — non-nutritive sweeteners, considered safe in pregnancy in normal amounts; moderate if she prefers to limit them. — swap: plain oats/whole-grain cereal if avoiding sweeteners
Suggested modifications
  • Have it with PRO yogurt + berries for a balanced, prenatally-useful breakfast (protein + fiber + vitamin C)
  • Allergen: peanuts (facility also handles milk + tree nuts)
