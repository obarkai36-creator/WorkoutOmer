Ingredients

  • ground chicken breast500 g
  • paprika1 tbsp
  • salt, pepperto taste
  • egg1 (for the wash)
  • chili (hot or mild)1 tbsp (in the wash)
  • soy sauce1 tbsp (in the wash)
  • breadcrumbs~40 g (coating; not quantified in recipe)
  • sesame seeds~15 g (coating; not quantified)

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Mix the ground chicken with the paprika, salt and pepper — season generously, chicken breast is bland. (For juicier balls, a spoon of breadcrumbs in the mix + resting 15 min helps them hold.)
  2. With wet hands, roll into even balls (even size = even baking); set on a tray.
  3. In a separate bowl, beat the egg with the chili, soy sauce and pepper.
  4. Dip each ball in the egg wash, then roll to coat in the breadcrumbs + sesame — the egg wash is what makes the crumb stick and crisp up.
  5. Space them out on a lined tray (crowding steams them soft); for max crisp without frying, set them on a wire rack over the tray and lightly spray/brush with oil.
  6. Bake at 200 C (400 F) ~20-25 min, turning once, until golden and cooked through (74 C, no pink). Panko crisps better than fine crumbs.
  7. Optional: broil/grill 1-2 min at the end for extra crunch. Rest a couple of minutes before serving.

One of the leanest, most protein-dense mains here — 32.5 g protein for just 235 kcal (~13.8 g protein per 100 kcal) because it's BAKED, not fried. Suggested modifications

  • Spray the balls lightly with oil + use panko for maximum crisp without frying
  • Serve over a big salad for fiber/lycopene/vitamin C
