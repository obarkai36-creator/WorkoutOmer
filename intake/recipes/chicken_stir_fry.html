For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
An outstanding cutting/muscle meal on the numbers — extremely lean and protein-dense (~49 g protein for ~408 kcal, ~12.9 g protein per 100 g), loaded with low-calorie high-volume veg, and carrying selenium + zinc (sperm micros). The only real knock is SODIUM: three salty sauces (soy + sweet soy + pad thai) stack to ~620 mg/serving, and the sweet soy/pad thai add some sugar. Refined glass noodles are a minor carb, small vs the protein/veg.
Highlights
  • very high protein density
  • high-volume low-calorie veg (satiety)
  • selenium + zinc (sperm micros)
  • high sodium (~620 mg/serving, 3 sauces)
  • refined glass noodles
Suggested modifications
  • Cut sodium: use low-sodium soy or drop the plain soy to 1 tbsp — you'll barely notice with sweet soy + pad thai already in
  • Measure any cooking oil (~1 tbsp) — it's the main hidden-calorie swing since none was listed
  • Already great; add more cabbage/sprouts for even more volume on a hungry day

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Good preconception profile — lean protein + choline (~128 mg), folate from the cabbage/sprouts/onion, selenium, and iron paired with the veg's vitamin C. Two advisories, both easy: cook the sprouts fully (raw sprouts are a pregnancy caution), and the sodium is on the high side.
Highlights
  • choline from the chicken (~128 mg/serving)
  • folate from cabbage + sprouts + onion
  • selenium; iron paired with vegetable vitamin C
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ Bean sprouts: raw/undercooked sprouts carry a bacterial risk (listeria/salmonella/E. coli) that matters in pregnancy. In this dish they should be cooked through, not tossed in raw at the very end. — swap: cook sprouts fully, or omit once actively trying/pregnant
  • ⚠ High sodium (~620 mg/serving from three sauces) — worth easing during pregnancy. — swap: low-sodium soy / less soy
Suggested modifications
  • Make sure the sprouts are cooked through
  • Use low-sodium soy to bring the salt down
  • Keep the cabbage/onion/sprouts — they're the folate here
