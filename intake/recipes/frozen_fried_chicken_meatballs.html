For him  6/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
A convenience protein, not a standout. 16 g protein / 204 kcal (~7.8 g protein per 100 kcal) is only okay — dragged down by frying (12 g fat) and ~8 g of filler carb (soy protein + breadcrumbs). On the plus side for your sperm goal, it's poultry (not processed red meat) and trans fat is ≤0.5 g. Sodium 440 mg/100 g is moderate. Beats the Swedish beef version on the fertility lens, loses on raw protein density.
Highlights
  • poultry base (better fertility profile than processed red meat)
  • trans fat ≤0.5 g/100 g
  • fried — 12 g fat/100 g
  • filler carbs (soy + breadcrumbs)
  • moderate sodium (440 mg)
Suggested modifications
  • Occasional convenience only — your homemade veg chicken meatballs (176 kcal, 15.5 g protein, no frying, no trans fat) beat it outright
  • Air-fry from frozen rather than pan-frying to avoid adding more oil
  • Serve over a big salad to offset the sodium/filler with fiber + micros

For her  5/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
An okay protein top-up but not nutrient-dense: fried, ~440 mg sodium, only trace fiber, and less iron than the beef version (~1 mg/100 g est.). Fully cooked/frozen, so safe when reheated through. Contains gluten, soy, celery; may contain sesame, mustard, egg. The beef version gives her more iron + protein — better for the preconception iron priority.
Highlights
  • 16 g protein per 100 g
  • poultry, leaner saturated fat than the beef version
Safety advisories
