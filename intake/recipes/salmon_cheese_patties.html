Ingredients: canned salmon (ideally bone-in), cottage cheese, Shkedi soft white cheese, eggs, breadcrumbs, onion, yellow cheese, salt, pepper

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Drain the canned salmon well; if it's bone-in, mash the soft bones right into the mix (extra calcium — you won't feel them).
  2. Flake the salmon and mix with the cheeses, eggs, breadcrumbs and grated onion; season with salt, pepper, a squeeze of lemon and fresh dill (dill loves salmon).
  3. If the mix is wet, add breadcrumbs until it holds; chill 15-20 min.
  4. Form patties and bake at 200 C (400 F) ~20 min, flipping once, until golden — or pan-fry for a crisper crust.
  5. Rest a few minutes before serving.
  6. Tip: a little lemon zest in the mix makes the salmon taste fresh instead of 'canned'.

For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
The better version for your sperm goal. You give up a little protein vs the tuna (26 vs 29 g/serving, still ~9 g protein per 100 kcal) and gain ~14 g of mostly-healthy fat — but that fat brings ~650 mg omega-3 EPA/DHA AND ~230 IU vitamin D per serving, TWO of your sperm-priority micros the tuna version barely had. Plus more calcium. Same cheese-driven sodium; yellow cheese is still the lever.
Highlights
  • omega-3 EPA/DHA ~650 mg/serving (sperm micro)
  • vitamin D ~230 IU (sperm micro)
  • still high protein (~9 g/100 kcal)
  • more calcium (bone-in salmon)
  • cheese-driven sodium (~400 mg)
Suggested modifications
  • Use bone-in canned salmon — the soft bones add calcium + vitamin D at no effort
  • Bake, not fry; drop/halve the yellow cheese to trim sodium/fat
  • Serve over greens for fiber + folate
