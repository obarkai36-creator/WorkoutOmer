For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Nearly identical to the cinnamon version and just as good on the headline macros: 20 g protein for 160 kcal, 0 g sugar, ~5 g net carb — a chocolate-dessert craving that fits a cut. One notch lower than the cinnamon only because it has less fiber (6 g vs 8 g) and MORE sugar alcohol (12 g erythritol vs 8 g), so it's the more likely of the two to cause bloating/GI if you eat more than one.
Highlights
  • 20 g protein / 160 kcal
  • 0 g sugar, ~5 g net carb
  • 12 g erythritol (higher GI/bloat risk)
  • 6 g fiber
  • ultra-processed
Suggested modifications
  • Great chocolate-craving swap; keep to 1 pack (12 g erythritol is a lot to double up on)
  • If choosing between the two Legendary donuts, the cinnamon crumble edges it (more fiber, less sugar alcohol)

For her  6/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Same good protein (20 g) and low sugar as the cinnamon, but the 12 g erythritol is a notable sugar-alcohol load — more likely to have a laxative/GI effect, which is worth being mindful of in pregnancy. No prenatal micros. Contains milk and egg.
Highlights
  • 20 g complete protein
Safety advisories
  • ⚠ High sugar-alcohol load (12 g erythritol) — can have a laxative/GI effect; sweeteners considered pregnancy-safe in moderation but this one is worth limiting. — swap: the cinnamon crumble version (less erythritol), or a whole-food snack
Suggested modifications
  • Occasional only; the cinnamon version is the gentler pick for her
  • Allergens: milk, egg
