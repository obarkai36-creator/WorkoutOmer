For him  5/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
Better macros than the chicken version — 19 g protein / 206 kcal (~9.2 g protein per 100 kcal) with only 3 g carb — but worse on the sperm-optimization lens: it's processed red meat, carries 0.6 g trans fat/100 g (trans fat is uniquely worth minimizing for fertility + heart), 4.5 g saturated fat, and 60 mg cholesterol. So it wins if pure cutting macros are the only goal, and loses once sperm optimization is weighted in.
Highlights
  • higher protein density (19 g, ~9.2 g/100 kcal)
  • low carb (3 g)
  • trans fat 0.6 g/100 g (real negative)
  • processed red meat (mild sperm negative)
  • sat fat 4.5 g, cholesterol 60 mg
Suggested modifications
  • If you buy one of the two, the trans fat + processed-red-meat combo makes this the worse pick for your sperm goal despite the better protein
  • Occasional only; homemade meatballs avoid the trans fat entirely
  • Air-fry from frozen; pair with veg for fiber

For her  6/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
For her, this edges out the chicken version: red meat brings more iron (~2.3 mg/100 g est.) and more protein (19 g) — both genuine preconception priorities, and the iron matters more than it does for him. The catch is the 0.6 g trans fat/100 g, which is worth minimizing in pregnancy, plus 440 mg sodium. Fully cooked/frozen — safe reheated through.
