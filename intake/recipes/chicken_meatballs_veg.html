Ingredients: ground chicken, onion, sweet potato, zucchini / courgette, egg, breadcrumbs, ketchup, salt, pepper

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Squeeze the grated sweet potato and zucchini hard in a clean towel to wring out the water — wet veg makes the mix loose and the meatballs fall apart.
  2. Fry the chopped onion in a little oil until soft and golden (~5 min), then let it cool — adding it hot starts cooking the raw mix.
  3. Mix chicken, cooled onion, grated veg, eggs, breadcrumbs and ketchup. Season boldly: chicken is bland, so be generous with salt, pepper and a little garlic and paprika.
  4. Chill the mix ~1 hour (30 min minimum) so it firms up and holds its shape.
  5. With wet hands, roll even golf-ball-sized meatballs (even size = even cooking).
  6. For the leanest result, bake at 200 C (400 F) for ~20-25 min; or pan-fry over medium, turning, until golden and cooked through (74 C, no pink inside).
  7. Rest a couple of minutes before serving so the juices settle.
  8. Tip: swap some breadcrumbs for oat bran to add fiber without changing the texture.

For him  8/10

weight loss · muscle retention · sperm
High protein density (~8.8 g protein / 100 kcal), low sugar, veg baked in — a strong cutting/muscle-retention dinner. Fat is a touch high from the frying, and fiber is low.
Highlights
  • high protein density
  • hidden vegetables
  • moderate zinc + selenium (sperm micros)
Suggested modifications
  • Swap ½ cup breadcrumbs for oat bran or ground oats to lift fiber toward the 32 g/day target
  • Bake or air-fry instead of pan-frying to cut the added fat
  • Serve over a big tomato/leafy salad — adds lycopene, vitamin C and fiber the dish itself is short on

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
Good lean protein plus iron and choline (from the eggs) — both preconception priorities. Folate is low as-is; easy to fix with greens. Fully cooked, no safety flags.
Highlights
  • iron from the chicken (pair with a vitamin-C side for absorption)
  • choline from the 2 eggs — commonly under-consumed preconception
Suggested modifications
  • Fold a couple of handfuls of chopped spinach into the mix — adds folate and more iron
  • Serve with a citrus or tomato side to boost non-heme iron absorption
