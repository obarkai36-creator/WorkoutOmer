🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, egg whites, peanut butter and honey in a blender/food processor until completely smooth — blending out the cottage lumps is what gives the fudgy, not-grainy texture.
  2. Add the cocoa powder, vanilla, baking powder and salt; blend again just until uniform and glossy.
  3. Pour into a parchment-lined 8×8 pan and level the top.
  4. Bake at 175 C (350 F) ~20-25 min — pull it when the top is set but the centre is still slightly soft (a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs). Overbaking makes protein brownies dry/rubbery, so err on the fudgy side.
  5. Cool fully in the pan, then chill 1-2 hours before cutting — they firm up a lot cold and slice cleanly into 9.
  6. Tip: a scoop of chocolate protein powder in the batter boosts protein and firms the texture; a spoon of cocoa nibs or a light swirl of extra peanut butter on top adds crunch/richness.

For her  7/10

preconception / prenatal (advisory)
A nice better-for-her treat: complete protein + calcium (cottage), plus iron and magnesium from the cocoa, and honey's antioxidants. Baked (eggs cooked), pasteurized cottage — no safety flags. One minor note: cocoa contains a little caffeine/theobromine (~8 mg caffeine per brownie), so a few brownies still sit well under the pregnancy caffeine limit — just count it if she's also having coffee.
