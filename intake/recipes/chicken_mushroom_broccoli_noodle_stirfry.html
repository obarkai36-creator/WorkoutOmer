🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Prep everything first — stir-fry moves fast. Soak the glass noodles in warm water until pliable (5-10 min), drain, and snip shorter with scissors.
  2. Steam or blanch the broccoli ~2 min (it's the densest veg), then drain — this way it cooks evenly and stays bright and crisp-tender.
  3. Pat the chicken strips dry and season lightly. Heat the pan/wok hot before the oil goes in — high heat sears, low heat steams.
  4. Stir-fry the chicken in a single layer until just cooked, then remove (crowding makes it grey and watery).
  5. Stir-fry the garlic and onion briefly, then the mushrooms — leave the mushrooms undisturbed a minute to brown (moving them too soon makes them release water and steam).
  6. Add the zucchini and blanched broccoli and toss 2-3 min to keep them crisp-tender.
  7. Return the chicken, add the noodles, and season to taste (salt/pepper, or a splash of low-sodium soy); toss 1-2 min to combine and heat through.
  8. Serve promptly. Tip: a squeeze of lemon or lime at the end lifts the whole dish.

Suggested modifications
