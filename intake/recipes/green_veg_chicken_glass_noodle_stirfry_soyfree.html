Ingredients: chicken breast, glass (mung bean) noodles, green beans, zucchini, broccoli, fresh spinach, green onion, fresh red chili, garlic, salt, black pepper, fresh lemon (or lime) juice, water, sesame oil, mild olive oil (for frying)

🍳 Cooking guide — for best taste & texture

  1. Prep everything first. Soak the glass noodles in warm water until pliable, drain, and snip shorter.
  2. Blanch or steam the broccoli and green beans ~2 min, then shock in cold water to keep them bright green and crisp.
  3. Pat the chicken dry and sear in a hot wok in a single layer until just cooked; remove and set aside.
  4. Stir-fry the garlic and chili briefly, then the zucchini and blanched greens over high heat 2-3 min.
  5. Add the noodles with the 1/4 cup water, salt, pepper and sesame oil, and toss — the water keeps the noodles from drying out.
  6. Return the chicken, then fold in the spinach and green onion until just wilted.
  7. Finish off the heat with the fresh lemon/lime juice — it brightens everything and boosts iron absorption. Serve promptly.
  8. Tip: salt to taste — you can go light and lean on the lemon, chili and garlic for flavor.
